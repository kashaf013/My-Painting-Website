ABOUT THE ARTIST

My Story

Hi, I'm Syeda Kashaf Zahra, a self-taught artist driven by passion and pure intuition.

My artistic journey started long before I ever touched a canvas, beginning with painting the walls of my home, crafting, and designing handmade posters. Over time, that creative energy naturally evolved into a dedicated love for painting.

Without any formal training, every piece I create is shaped by practice, personal vision, and genuine emotion. For me, art is a way of capturing thoughts and feelings that words cannot quite express.

Thank you for visiting my gallery.
